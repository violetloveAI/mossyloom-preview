# Mossyloom demo photography

Prepared 2026-10-02 from the user-directed supplier research already in this workspace.

- 36 catalog entries: 16 scarves, 10 hats, 7 gloves, 3 simulated pairings.
- 33 individual supplier products and 87 distinct real photographs.
- Each photograph has a full image up to 1200 px and a 480 px thumbnail. The 174 optimized WebP files total approximately 10.5 MB.
- Ten individual products have multi-image galleries. Eight have two or more visually matched colour choices.
- No synthetic product photographs were generated. Image processing was limited to orientation, resizing and WebP compression.
- `catalog.json` is the primary catalog; `catalog.js` makes the same data available as `window.MOSSYLOOM_CATALOG` without fetching.
- `product-photo-index.csv` lists the product name, category, first image and supplier page.
- `image-provenance.json` records the original local file and supplier page for every full photograph.

## Source and rights record

All included images come from the already collected listing photography of 桐庐愿望针织有限公司 at <https://tlywfz.1688.com/>. The prior “1688进货相关” chat explicitly requested collecting the full-store main images and recommended-product detail images, and using them for independent-store design proposals. On 2026-10-02, the user explicitly confirmed “已获得允许，使用供应商实拍图” for this publicly accessible demonstration. This records the user's permission statement for the current public demo; it does not assert ownership, a perpetual license, or unrestricted permanent commercial use.

Images from Chinti & Parker and GOBI in `tmp/pdfs/assets` were deliberately excluded; no reuse license was found for those unrelated brands.

## Demo data

Retail prices are invented USD values solely for interaction testing. Names describe the visible form and colour. Material percentages, fiber origins, certifications, genuine stock, reviews, sales claims, delivery promises and verified sizing are not asserted. The three Sets are simulated pairings and display photographs of their individual components.
